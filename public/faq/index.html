FAQ

Read the fine print (there isn't any).

Click any question to expand. The answers are short on purpose — if you want detail, follow the links to the relevant page.

Is this really free?

Yes. Completely free. No catch, no upsell, no billing surprise. The program is funded by its co-founding partners (Chicago Fleet Wraps and Regions PM) plus future participating partners. The bet on their end: businesses we help in their first 90 days often come back when they need full-scale services years later.

What do I get?

Two services to start. From Chicago Fleet Wraps: free design and installation of vehicle graphics (up to 8 sq ft of cast vinyl) on your business vehicle. From Regions PM: a free 1-hour property search consultation if you need office, retail, or warehouse space in Chicagoland.

Who qualifies?

Any Chicagoland business with a license, LLC formation, or DBA registration dated within the last 90 days. Service area: Cook, DuPage, Lake, Kane, Will, and McHenry Counties. See full eligibility →

What if my license is more than 90 days old?

The 90-day window is firm. The program is specifically designed to help businesses in their critical first quarter. If you're past 90 days, contact our partners directly — Chicago Fleet Wraps offers fleet pricing with a small-business discount, and Regions PM offers property consultations to all clients.

Can my business become a partner?

Yes. We're actively onboarding partners across categories: attorneys, accountants, banks, payroll providers, web designers, printers, marketing agencies, insurance brokers. See partner program →

Why does this exist?

Most new businesses spend their first 90 days on the basics — getting licensed, opening a business bank account, finding their first customer. Marketing, branding, and operational essentials come last. The New Business Project covers the first piece of professional infrastructure so new businesses look legitimate from day one. More from the founders →

Do I need to upload my license to apply?

Not at the application stage. The 5-field form just asks for your license date and county. After we confirm preliminary eligibility (within two business hours), we ask for a photo or scan of your license document so we can verify before scheduling work.

How long does the vehicle graphics install take?

30 to 90 minutes in the CFW Chicago bay (4711 N Lamon Ave). Most recipients drop the vehicle off, grab coffee nearby, and drive away with a branded vehicle in under two hours. Installs typically happen within two weeks of approval.

Can I bring my own design?

Yes. Vector files (AI, EPS, SVG) are preferred. PNG or JPEG work too. If you have nothing yet, CFW's in-house designers will produce 2–3 layout options for your approval before printing — design is included at no cost.

What if I don't need both services?

Take only what helps. The application has checkboxes for "vehicle graphics", "property search", or both. We route you to the partner(s) you actually need — there's no requirement to take both, and no penalty for taking just one.

Got more questions?

Email us. We respond within two business hours during business days.
