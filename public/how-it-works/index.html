The process

From application to finished install in about two weeks.

Each step has a clear handoff and a real human responsible for it. No automation, no chatbots, no "your application is being reviewed" loop.

01

Apply

Fill out the five-field application: name, business name, license date, county, and which services you want. Five minutes total. Submit it and you'll see a confirmation page within seconds.

02

Verify & coordinate

Within two business hours, a real person reads your application. We confirm eligibility (license date + Chicagoland address) and route you to the right partner(s). You'll get an email with next steps.

03

Get started

Graphics: drop your vehicle at the CFW Chicago bay (4711 N Lamon Ave) for 30–90 minutes. Property: a Regions PM advisor schedules a 1-hour consultation call within a week. Done.

What to expect at each step

Detail. Step by step.

STEP 1 · APPLY

Five-field application

The form asks for the absolute minimum we need: your name, business name, email, phone, license date, county, and which services would help you. There's an optional "anything we should know" field for notes about your vehicle, your space needs, your logo files, your timeline.

You don't need to upload your license document at this point. We ask for that later if eligibility looks good. We don't want to slow you down at the front of the funnel.

Time required

Five minutes if you have your license date handy. Two if you've got it memorized.

STEP 2 · VERIFY

Real human review

A real person — not an algorithm — reads your application within two business hours during business days. We check eligibility (date + address), look at any notes you left, and decide which partner(s) to route you to.

You'll get an email confirming eligibility and asking for the one document we need: a photo or scan of your business license. From there we connect you with the right founding partner(s) for next steps.

Turnaround

Two business hours typical. Same business day always.

STEP 3A · GRAPHICS

Vehicle graphics install

Chicago Fleet Wraps schedules your install. They'll work with you on design — bring your own logo files (vector preferred — AI, EPS, SVG) or start from scratch with their in-house designers, who produce 2–3 layout options for your approval before printing.

The install itself takes 30–90 minutes in a heated, climate-controlled bay. Drop the vehicle off, grab coffee nearby, drive away with a branded vehicle. Most installs happen within two weeks of approval.

Where

CFW Chicago bay · 4711 N Lamon Ave, Chicago IL 60630

STEP 3B · PROPERTY

Property search consultation

Regions PM schedules a 1-hour consultation call (phone or video, your choice) within a week. They walk you through current listings matched to your needs, neighborhood-level rent benchmarks, and lease term guidance.

You leave the call with a packet of properties to consider, market context, and a real understanding of what your money actually buys in Chicagoland's commercial real estate market.

Format

1-hour call (phone or video) · Listings packet emailed after

Ready to start?

The application takes five minutes. The whole process takes about two weeks.
