Why this exists

You spent your first 90 days on the boring stuff.

Filing the LLC with the Illinois Secretary of State. Opening a business checking account. Getting your EIN from the IRS. Calling your insurance broker. Picking a name your aunt won't hate. Driving an unmarked Ford Transit around Chicago that nobody knows belongs to a real business yet.

Marketing comes last. Always does. By the time you can afford branding, you've already lost a few customers who didn't know you existed. We're trying to fix that. A vehicle graphics company and a property management firm pay for your first piece of professional infrastructure — so you don't have to wait six months to look like a real business.

If your business is anywhere in the six-county Chicagoland metro — Cook, DuPage, Lake, Kane, Will, or McHenry County — you qualify. The 90-day clock is the only filter.

Who qualifies

Four rules. That's the whole list.

No income test. No industry filter. No "must be a woman-owned, minority-owned, veteran-owned" requirement (though all of those are absolutely welcome). If you meet these four, you're in.

3

No income test. No industry filter. No application essay. Two minutes to apply. Approval typically in two business hours during business days.

Common questions

Quick answers.

Is this really free?

Yes. Completely free. The program is funded by Chicago Fleet Wraps and Regions PM out of their marketing budgets. The bet on their end: businesses we help in their first 90 days remember it. Some come back two or three years later when they need a real $5,000 wrap or want to renegotiate a lease. That's the whole model. No fine print, no upsell. More questions →

Where exactly do you serve?

The six-county Chicagoland metro: Cook, DuPage, Lake, Kane, Will, and McHenry counties in Illinois. Combined population roughly 8.4 million.

What if my license is more than 90 days old?

The 90-day window is firm. The program is designed for that critical first quarter. If you're past 90, contact our partners directly — Chicago Fleet Wraps offers fleet pricing with a small-business discount, and Regions PM consults paying clients at standard rates. See full FAQ →
