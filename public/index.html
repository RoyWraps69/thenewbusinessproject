Why this exists

Most new businesses spend their first 90 days on the basics.

Getting licensed. Opening a business bank account. Finding the first customer. Locking down a place to work from. Marketing comes last — usually long after the truck has been driving around unmarked for months and the founder has been working from a kitchen table for half a year. The New Business Project covers two of the first essentials — a branded vehicle and a real workspace — so when your business shows up to job number one, it looks legitimate from day one.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is this really free?

Yes. Completely free. No catch, no upsell, no billing surprise. The program is funded by its co-founding partners (Chicago Fleet Wraps and Regions PM) plus future participating partners. The bet on their end: businesses we help in their first 90 days often come back when they need full-scale services years later. That's the entire model.

What do I get?

Two services to start. From Chicago Fleet Wraps: free design and installation of vehicle graphics (up to 8 sq ft of cast vinyl) on your business vehicle. From Regions PM: a free 1-hour property search consultation if you need office, retail, or warehouse space in Chicagoland. Additional services will be added as more partners join.

Who qualifies?

Any Chicagoland business with a license, LLC formation, or DBA registration dated within the last 90 days. Service area: Cook, DuPage, Lake, Kane, Will, and McHenry Counties. No income tests, no industry restrictions, no application essays.

What if my license is more than 90 days old?

The 90-day window is firm. The program is specifically designed to help businesses in their critical first quarter. If you're past 90 days, contact our partners directly — Chicago Fleet Wraps offers fleet pricing with a small-business discount, and Regions PM offers property consultations to all clients.

Can my business become a partner?

Yes. We're actively onboarding partners across categories: attorneys, accountants, banks, payroll providers, web designers, printers, marketing agencies, insurance brokers. As a partner, you offer one specific service to recipients at no charge or significantly reduced cost, and you're credited prominently. Email partners@thenewbusinessproject.com to apply.
