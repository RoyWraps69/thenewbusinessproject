Why this exists

Most new businesses spend their first 90 days on the basics.

Getting licensed. Opening a business bank account. Finding the first customer. Locking down a place to work from. Marketing comes last. Usually long after the truck has been driving around unmarked for months and the founder has been working from a kitchen table for half a year. The New Business Project covers two of the first essentials — a branded vehicle and a real workspace — so when your business shows up to job number one, it looks legitimate from day one.

About the program

The New Business Project was co-founded in 2024 by Chicago Fleet Wraps and Regions PM to help new Chicagoland businesses with their first essential services — a branded vehicle and a real workspace. The program is funded by its co-founding partners and is structured to scale by onboarding additional partners.

Recipients receive free services with no income test, no industry filter, no application essay. Eligibility is simple: a Chicagoland address and a business license dated within the last 90 days.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is this really free?

Yes. Completely free. No catch, no upsell, no billing surprise. The program is funded by its co-founding partners (Chicago Fleet Wraps and Regions PM) plus future participating partners. The bet on their end: businesses we help in their first 90 days often come back when they need full-scale services years later.

What do I get?

Two services to start. From Chicago Fleet Wraps: free design and installation of vehicle graphics (up to 8 sq ft of cast vinyl) on your business vehicle. From Regions PM: a free 1-hour property search consultation if you need office, retail, or warehouse space in Chicagoland.

Who qualifies?

Any Chicagoland business with a license, LLC formation, or DBA registration dated within the last 90 days. Service area: Cook, DuPage, Lake, Kane, Will, and McHenry Counties.

What if my license is more than 90 days old?

The 90-day window is firm. The program is specifically designed to help businesses in their critical first quarter. If you're past 90 days, contact our partners directly — Chicago Fleet Wraps offers fleet pricing with a small-business discount, and Regions PM offers property consultations to all clients.

Can my business become a partner?

Yes. We're actively onboarding partners across categories: attorneys, accountants, banks, payroll providers, web designers, printers, marketing agencies, insurance brokers. Email partners@thenewbusinessproject.com to apply.

Why does this exist?

Most new businesses spend their first 90 days on the basics — getting licensed, opening a business bank account, finding their first customer. Marketing, branding, and operational essentials come last. The New Business Project covers the first piece of professional infrastructure so new businesses look legitimate from day one.
