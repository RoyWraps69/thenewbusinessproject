For service businesses

Nine categories. Open to apply.

If you fall into any of these categories and operate in Chicagoland, we want to hear from you. The categories below are the ones where new businesses need help most in their first 90 days.

Attorneys (LLC formation) Accountants & bookkeepers Banks (business checking) Payroll providers Web designers & developers Print shops Marketing agencies Insurance brokers Commercial real estate

As a partner, you offer one specific service to program recipients at no charge or significantly reduced cost. In return:

  • Credited prominently Logo and link on our public partner directory and on each recipient's welcome materials.
  • Direct relationships Recipients know who's helping them. The strongest customer relationships start with someone who showed up first.
  • Vetted by the program Partners are reviewed for quality and reputation. We don't accept anyone — that protects your reputation alongside ours.

How partner onboarding works

Three steps. About a week.

Same kind of process as our applicant flow — designed to be fast and direct.

01

Email us

Tell us who you are, what category you'd serve, and what specific service you'd offer to recipients at no or reduced cost. One email is enough to start.

02

Quality review

We check your reputation, online reviews, time in business, and references if needed. Partners are vetted because the program's reputation rides on yours and vice versa.

03

Onboard

If approved, you're added to the partner directory, your service is added to the recipient welcome kit, and we coordinate handoffs as new applications come in.

Ready to partner?

Email us with your business name, category, and what you'd offer. We respond within two business hours.
